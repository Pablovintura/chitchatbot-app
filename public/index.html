It runs on your side

Install it, open a stream, and foreign messages get a line under them in the color of the site you're on. The first time you need a language, click the bar above the chat box and your browser downloads it once.

Messages in your own language, links, commands and emote-only messages are left alone.

12:20lena_de:kann man das auch auf deutsch machen?
Download German to translate this chat. Click to start.
Send a message

Write in your language

Turn on write mode and type like normal. A preview above the box shows what will be sent, and you pick: send the translation, or send it as you wrote it.

It works out the chat's language on its own. If it guesses wrong, you choose one.

12:17kejosrt:hola desde Mexico, saludos

Hola, ¿cómo están todos?

No account, nothing sent anywhere

Translation uses the translator built into Chrome and Brave, so your chat never goes to a server of ours. There's no sign-up and no tracking. The privacy page says exactly what it reads and keeps.

It handles English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian, Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. YouTube works in the pop-out chat too.
