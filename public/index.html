HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, then forget about it

  1. 1

    Add it

    Install the extension in Chrome or Brave and pin it to your toolbar.

  2. 2

    Pick your language

    The first time a language is needed, click the bar above the chat box. Each language downloads once.

  3. 3

    Just watch

    Messages in other languages get a translation under them, in the color of the site you are on.

Twitch

YouTube

Red lines in live chat, including the pop-out chat window.

PRIVATE

Nothing leaves your computer

Translation uses the translator built into your browser. We don't run a server for it, there are no accounts and no analytics. Read the privacy page.
