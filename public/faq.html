FAQ

How do I install it?

The Chrome Web Store listing is coming soon. Until then you can install it by hand in about a minute:

  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or brave://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped chitchatbot-extension folder.
  4. Open any Kick or Twitch channel, or a YouTube live stream, and refresh the page. Pin ChitChatBot from the puzzle-piece menu to reach its settings.

Using it

Nothing is translating

Refresh the stream tab after installing or updating. Your browser needs the built-in translator (Chrome 138 or newer, or a recent Brave) on a desktop computer. Messages in your own language, very short messages and emote-only messages are skipped on purpose.

A green bar asks me to download a language

Each language pair needs a one-time download. Click the bar once and wait for it to finish. It will not ask again for that pair.

Why is a translation a bit off?

The on-device translator is quick and private but weaker on slang and jokes. A higher-quality option may come later.

How do I hide the original text?

Open the ChitChatBot menu and choose "Translation only" under Reading.

How does Write mode work?

Turn on "Write in my language" in the menu. When you type in the chat box, a preview shows your message in the chat's language. Press Enter to send it, or choose "Send as I wrote it". Commands that start with / or !, very short messages and messages with emotes are sent as typed.

Does it work on phones?

Not yet. It needs a desktop browser.

Is it affiliated with Kick, Twitch or YouTube?

No. ChitChatBot is an independent project and only changes how chat looks on your own screen.
