Privacy

Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version

There are two parts. The viewer extension works on your computer and sends nothing about you or your chat to us. The Kick bot is for streamers: it runs on our server, reads your chat to translate it, and never saves what anyone wrote. We have no analytics.

The viewer extension

  • Chat messages on kick.com, twitch.tv, tiktok.com LIVE, rumble.com and YouTube live chat, so it can translate them for you.
  • If you turn on Write mode, the text you type in the chat box, so it can show a translated preview and send it.

It only runs on kick.com, twitch.tv, tiktok.com LIVE, rumble.com and YouTube live chat pages and does not read any other site.

Where translation happens

Translation and language detection use the translator built into Chrome and Brave, which runs on your device. When you use a language pair for the first time, your browser downloads that language pack from its own provider. That download is made by your browser, not by ChitChatBot.

What is stored

Your settings (language, switches) are saved with the browser's extension storage. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome may sync these settings across your devices. Chat messages and translations are kept in memory while the page is open and are not saved.

The bot (for streamers)

  • Signing in. You sign in with Kick or Twitch. We never see your password. For Kick we keep your Kick user id, your channel name and the access tokens Kick gives us, which are stored encrypted, so the bot can read and post in your chat. For Twitch we keep only your Twitch user id and channel name, and no token: the bot posts from its own account.
  • Chat. Kick or Twitch sends your channel's chat messages to our server. Messages are checked in memory. Only messages in another language are sent to Cloudflare (Workers AI) to be translated, then the translation is posted in your chat. Chat messages, usernames and translations are not saved or logged by us.
  • What is saved. Your settings (switch, language, pause, mods option) and how many characters were translated each month, which is a number only.
  • Leaving. "Disconnect" on a channel in the dashboard deletes the saved tokens and turns the bot off for it.

Bug reports

If you send a bug report from the report page, it is emailed to us exactly as you typed it: what broke, what happened and your email if you added one. Nothing else is collected with it, and we don't keep a copy on the site. We use it to fix the bug and to reply if you asked us to.

Support payments

The Support link opens a payment page run by Stripe in a new tab. Payment details are entered on Stripe's page and handled by Stripe under its own privacy policy. ChitChatBot never sees your card details. The extension and this site do not use cookies or analytics.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@chitchatbot.stream

Changes

The viewer extension will never send your text to a ChitChatBot server without a separate opt-in. If anything about the bot changes, this page is updated first.
