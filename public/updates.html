Updates

New stuff and fixes for the bot and the extension. Each one gets its own entry, so you only read what you use.

  1. Bot1.0.0
    • Translates chat for you. Anything not in your language gets a line underneath. No more nodding along.
    • On and off from the dashboard, or type !translate on and !translate off in chat.
    • A skip list for chatters you'd rather leave alone. Other bots, mostly.
    • Make the bot's line look how you like: flag or no flag, the language in brackets, or the original message tacked on for proof.
    • Both Kick and Twitch linked? Each has its own switch. !translate off quiets one chat, !translate off all quiets both.
    • Works on Kick and Twitch. Got both? Link both and flip between them on one dashboard.
    • Free: 50,000 characters a month. Your chat isn't saved.
  2. Extension1.0.1
    • The Support button now takes you to Ko-fi. Same tip jar, nicer door.
  3. Extension1.0.0
    • Translation under every message on Kick, Twitch, YouTube, TikTok LIVE and Rumble. Yes, even the ones you were pretending to understand.
    • Write back in their language, with a preview before it goes out.
    • It figures out what language you're typing in, so you can type in anything and send in anything. Show-off.
    • Enter sends the translation, Shift+Enter sends what you typed. One tap swaps them, for the chaotic.
    • Switched off? The icon goes grey, so you know it's napping.
    • Emotes survive the trip, 7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ included.
    • A tiny language flag on each translation, and a skip list of your own.
